Privacy

Effective November 17, 2026 · PartyLine 0.4.5

This notice describes Chad Owen's personal PartyLine app and its Google Calendar connection. Contact heychad@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Google account and calendar data

With your consent, PartyLine reads your Google account identifier and verified email address, your calendar list, and events in calendars you select. Event suggestions include titles, start and end times, attendee names or email addresses, and supported meeting links. It requests read-only calendar-list and event permissions, plus sign-in identity. It cannot create, change, or delete Google events.

Account identifiers, email addresses, calendar names, and selections are stored in the app's local settings. OAuth tokens and the client secret are stored in macOS Keychain. Event suggestions are cached in memory. Attaching an event saves a snapshot with that recording; copying its title and invitees into meeting details is optional. Invitees are suggestions, not confirmed speaker identities.

Cloud processing and sharing

Calendar suggestions are not sent to AssemblyAI or OpenAI merely because you connect an account. If you copy event details into a recording, its title and resulting transcript or notes can be included in OpenAI processing. Participant names can be sent to AssemblyAI as transcription vocabulary hints.

Cloud transcription sends captured audio to AssemblyAI. Explicit transcript recovery uploads the selected saved audio channel. Notes send transcripts and existing notes to OpenAI; recording chat sends your question and retrieved transcript passages with their source titles. These services process requests under your own provider accounts and their policies.

If you enable Drive backup and choose a folder, PartyLine copies audio and text there; Google Drive for desktop handles cloud synchronization. Exports, clipboard copies, and agents you authorize may receive meeting content. Keep those copies and connected tools in mind when sharing or removing data.

PartyLine uses Google user data only for these requested features. It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. PartyLine's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

Local recordings, transcripts, notes, chat history, and edit revisions remain on your Mac until removed. Trash and redaction are recoverable controls: they do not permanently erase originals, prior revisions, exports, or backup copies. Redaction limits sharing through PartyLine's edited exports.

Remove an account in Calendar to delete its local credentials and current event suggestions. Existing recording attachments remain. Revoke PartyLine's Google access separately in Google Account connections. Remove local files and backups separately when you want them deleted; provider retention and deletion are controlled by the provider accounts.

Diagnostics and website hosting

Optional inherited usage analytics are off by default. If enabled, they use the upstream Meetily PostHog service for app usage and device information. Google Calendar data is not included in calendar analytics events. Local diagnostic files can contain operational errors and meeting identifiers.

This informational website is hosted by GitHub Pages and has no added analytics scripts or sign-in form. GitHub handles hosting request data under its privacy statement.
