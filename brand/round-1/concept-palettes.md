# PartyLine concept palettes

Round 1 color ideas. The speaker colors have separate light and dark variants.

Contrast is calculated against white (`#FFFFFF`) and near-black (`#171C1B`).

## Switchboard

Two colored patch cords share one jack on a pocket-sized telephone exchange.

| Role | Light hex | Light HSL | Contrast | Dark hex | Dark HSL | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brand | `#235553` | `178 42% 24%` | 8.41:1 | `#90C2BA` | `170 29% 66%` | 8.71:1 |
| ME | `#214E70` | `206 54% 28%` | 8.79:1 | `#8ABAD4` | `201 46% 69%` | 8.25:1 |
| THEM | `#976810` | `39 81% 33%` | 4.88:1 | `#F5CE7B` | `41 86% 72%` | 11.49:1 |
