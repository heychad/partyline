# PartyLine concepts

Three original SVG concepts for selection. The full brand set follows the chosen direction.

- **Switchboard:** Two colored patch cords share one jack on a pocket-sized telephone exchange.
- **Handset:** A two-tone receiver gives ME and THEM opposite ends of the same call.
- **Script:** Alternating dialogue lines connect through one looping telephone cord.

Each folder contains an editable `app-icon.svg`, a 1024 px PNG master, and 128, 32, and 16 px SVG and PNG exports. The small exports use the same artwork; no size-specific tuning has been applied.

The 1024 px canvas has a centered 824 px body with a 185 px corner radius and 100 px transparent margins. Every PNG has an sRGB profile and a real alpha channel. The icons contain no text, fonts, external images, or stock artwork.

`concept-palettes.md` lists light and dark speaker colors, HSL values, and calculated text contrast. Every speaker color exceeds 4.5:1 against its stated background. Grayscale appearance still needs the owner's visual judgment.

`source-brief.md` preserves the complete brief supplied for this round. No app assets have been replaced.
