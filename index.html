Your meetings, recorded.

PartyLine is Chad Owen's personal macOS meeting app, built from Meetily. It records microphone and Mac audio, transcribes with AssemblyAI, and creates notes and answers with OpenAI.

Connect your calendars

Connect multiple Google accounts, choose calendars, and attach an event's title and invitees to a recording. Calendar access is read-only. PartyLine never changes your Google events or starts recording automatically.

Keep control

Recordings are saved on your Mac. Cloud processing uses your own API connections. Optional backups use a folder you choose in Google Drive for desktop. Local agent tools require access granted in PartyLine.

This is a personal project, not a hosted recording service. See the source code.
